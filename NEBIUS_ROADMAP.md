# Nebius edition workflow and release checklist

The product centers on a project goal, a selected task, an optional bounded tool build, reviewable evidence and a separate human completion decision. Manual workflows and bundled examples are available without provider credentials.

Before release, verify the exact source and setup, the hosted access path, configured provider limits, expected tool behavior, keyboard and narrow-screen flows, and the submitted video and instructions. A local test, recorded example or older deployment does not establish a new hosted result.

Model-generated checks are not independent acceptance. Preserve failed and uncertain observations and identify any assistant-authored repairs separately from generated output.
